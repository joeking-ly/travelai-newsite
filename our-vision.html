Our Vision

One vision, written down
as we go.

We've been building toward the same future for years: travel that knows you, works for you, and remembers you without taking you away from the trip itself. Along the way we've written down what we see coming, at the moment we saw it. Each paper below is a marker in time. Read together, they trace a single line: build the engine, understand the shift, and give the traveler the memory that makes it all personal.

The thesis

Travel is becoming agentic, and the layer that lasts is memory.

AI agents are about to mediate how people plan, book, and manage travel. That much is no longer in question. The open question is what those agents read, who holds it, and whose interests it serves. We believe the answer is a portable memory layer that travelers own, and that the company best positioned to build it is one already operating at scale across the real travel economy.

That's us. We run a live network of 530+ travel brands. The network is two things at once: distribution that reaches millions of high intent travelers, and a proving ground where we apply AI to real bookings, in production, every day. The brands are how we learn what actually makes travel better. The memory layer is how we make that learning belong to the traveler.

The vision papers

Moments in time

A series of moments in time, each capturing where we stood and where we were headed. The direction has held; the picture keeps getting sharper.

  1. 01
    Paper 01: the virtual factory

    Building Our Factory of the Future

    October 2024 · Painted Picture

    The engine

    Before there was a memory layer, there had to be a machine that could learn. This paper describes the operating philosophy behind TravelAI: a virtual factory that builds the thing that builds the thing. Automate the 80% of work that every business shares, so our people, and now our agents, can pour themselves into the 20% that's ours alone. Break big things into small things and hand the repeatable ones to agents that never sleep. The point was never automation for its own sake. It was to build an engine efficient enough to make travel better for a billion people, and human enough to keep its soul.

    "Our job, and humanity's job, is to infuse the soul into the machine."
    Read the paper →
  2. 02
    Paper 02: the Third Voice

    The Third Voice

    February 2025 · The AI Power Shift

    The shift

    If the factory was about how we build, the Third Voice is about what's changing in the market we build for. Travel has always been a two sided marketplace: travelers and suppliers. AI introduces a third participant: agents that don't just assist, but decide, negotiate, and act. We argued that whoever holds this Third Voice will shape the customer experience for the next decade, and that it should be aligned to the same goal travelers and suppliers already share: booking better travel. The real question isn't who will own it. It's whether it serves travelers, not the incumbents.

    "Soon, you won't just search for travel. Travel will come to you."
    Read the paper →
  3. 03
    Paper 03: Traveler.md

    Traveler.md

    The portable memory layer

    The traveler

    Capable agents are here. But every one of them is an amnesiac. Each conversation starts from zero, each preference explained again. We call it the amnesia tax, and every traveler pays it. This paper makes the case for the missing layer: a portable, human readable memory the traveler owns and grants to any agent, app, or service they choose. Traveler.md is who you are. Trip.md is what you're planning. The connected trip isn't a journey assembled inside one platform. It's a trip that stays connected to the person, so every service starts from the same memory and gives it back better.

    "We believe a traveler's memory should travel with them."
    Read the paper →

The bigger vision

Help one billion travelers by building the founding travel AI infrastructure, owned by the traveler, governed for the enterprise, and open to all.

Three papers, one arc. The factory taught us how to operate AI at scale across real travel brands. The Third Voice told us where the market was going. Traveler.md is what we give the traveler so the agentic age works in their favor, not at their expense. The agent layer will be competitive and fast moving. The memory layer underneath should be cooperative, stable, and the traveler's. That's the layer we're building.

Why we acquired Sonder

The name is the thesis.

Sonder is the realization that every passing stranger has an inner life as vivid and complex as your own. It's the most precise word in travel for what we're building: a company that treats every traveler's tastes, history, and memories as singular, worth remembering, and worth carrying from one trip to the next. We didn't acquire a portfolio of buildings. We acquired the one brand in travel whose name already means what we mean.

Sonder is the most visible expression of the thesis these papers describe, and a clear example of how our brands work. Each brand gives us two things: distribution to high intent travelers, and a proving ground for applying AI to make travel better. Sonder relaunches at Sonder.com as a curated guide to the world's best urban stays: design led apartment style and boutique accommodations, chosen for taste rather than served as infinite, undifferentiated choice. Stays are fulfilled through our distribution partners. It's a continuation, not a teardown. We're keeping what made Sonder Sonder, and moving its technology from the lock on the door to discovery, memory, and personalization.

This is a path we know. We've acquired and revitalized Casai, Smartours, and OwnerDirect by pairing trusted travel names with modern distribution and applied AI. Sonder joins a portfolio we already know how to build, launch, and grow.

And it's where the memory thesis becomes real for travelers. Traveler.md, our traveler owned memory file, is in public beta and already being used to make trips more personal. Over time, we intend to make Sonder the flagship showcase for personalization led by memory: an experience designed to help the right traveler find the right place, and to remember them, with their permission, when they return. Our principle is simple. We never tell a traveler something they didn't share with us first.

On Sonder's wind down: TravelAI is a separate company. We acquired only the Sonder brand and its domains, through a separate transaction, after the operating company's closure, with no leases, no properties, no staff, and no assumed liabilities. We have real sympathy for the guests, residents, and employees affected, and direct any questions about past bookings or claims to the former company's insolvency proceedings.

Where this is going

Today, travel is a logistics job with a vacation hidden inside it. We think that's backwards. When your memory is portable and the agents around you can read it, the friction collapses: the rebooking, the visa form, the preference explained for the hundredth time. You get the hours back. Not the abstract hours of "saved time." The real ones, the ones where you sit down somewhere unfamiliar and remember why you came.

We're at the beginning. The foundation is small on purpose, and we're building it in the open, with hard problems named, not hidden. We are The Travel Memory Company, and we're excited for what's up next.
