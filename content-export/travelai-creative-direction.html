Travel. Memory. Company.

A homepage built on four mechanics borrowed from the Wolverine Worldwide reference, rebuilt in TravelAI's own system: cinematic hero video, a living portfolio of travel stories, a pinned horizontal journey through the four pillars, and a nav that gets out of the way.

01 · The reference, decoded

Four mechanics worth taking. Nothing else.

The Wolverine page works because every showpiece carries information: the video proves the brand is physical, the scatter proves breadth, the horizontal scroll forces sequence, the nav defers to content. We take the mechanics and swap in our meaning.

MECHANIC A

Full-bleed hero video + stacked declarative type

Their "Make. Every Day. Better." is three beats of display type, left-aligned over motion, with a small latest-news card anchored bottom right.

Ours: brand video (Veo shot set) under "Travel. Memory. Company." News card slot holds the Sonder announcement from July 20.
MECHANIC B

Scatter portfolio around a centered headline

Small photo chips drift at different scroll speeds around "A portfolio built for every step." Breadth shown, not claimed.

Ours: travel photography and story cards from the network orbiting "A network built for every trip." 530+ brands, felt.
MECHANIC C

Pinned horizontal scroll section

The page pins, scroll input translates panels sideways through a fixed sequence, then releases back to vertical flow.

Ours: this is where the four pillars live. Memory → Governance → Trust → Distribution. The mechanic enforces the argument's order.
MECHANIC D

Nav that condenses on scroll

Full-height transparent nav at top; past the hero it shrinks, gains a blur backdrop, and stays fixed.

Ours: identical behavior in ink glass. You are experiencing it in this document right now.

06 · System, assets, build

Same system, new theater.

TokenDirection
SurfacesInk-first default. Cream reserved for the scatter portfolio, the Trust panel, and Partners, so each cream moment reads as editorial relief.
TypeInter Tight 800 for display (tight tracking, stacked beats), Fraunces italic for the single emphasized word per headline. Never both treatments in body copy.
Spectrum gradientOnce per viewport maximum: hero "Company." + the AI in the wordmark. It is a signature, not a paint bucket.
PhotographyReal trips, warm grade, people mid-moment. No stock gloss, no passports, no suitcases.
Hero videoCut from the Veo brand shot set: arrival moments, not montage. 10 to 14s loop, no text baked in.
BuildGSAP ScrollTrigger for pin + scrub (battle-tested, IE-free). Native position: sticky for nav. Lazy-load everything below S3. Target LCP under 2.5s with the video poster as LCP element.
Hero headline decision needed: "Travel. Memory. Company." (category, recommended) vs "Portable. Governed. Better." (the three taglines). Both shown to team for reaction.
News card is timing-dependent: the Sonder slot goes live with the July 20 release, placeholder until then. Forward-looking, needs your sign-off on wording.
Performance vs press crawl: wire services and AI bots index on July 20. All pillar copy must exist in the DOM as real text, not canvas or image, for SEO and LLM readability.
GMP tense on the Governance panel follows whatever you decide for the Vision page (early-development qualifier vs current offering). Panel copy above is written tense-neutral so it works either way.
