05 · Motion & build spec

One showpiece. Everything else quiet.

Pinned pillars are the bold move. Portfolio parallax is secondary. Nav condense is constant. Use the stack that survives contact with reality.

LayerSpec
Smooth scrollLenis site-wide on concept homepage. lenis.on('scroll', ScrollTrigger.update) + GSAP ticker RAF. Disable when prefers-reduced-motion.
Nav condenseGSAP scrub first 140–160px scroll. --nav-h 72→52px, logo 36→24px, padding tightens, --nav-bg-alpha 0→0.96, blur 16px. Rainbow rule fades in.
Hero videoExisting YouTube embed. Poster fallback. No text baked into video. Type reveals after load (optional, subtle).
PortfolioPin stage ~1.8 viewport-heights. Chips: cluster at center → spread on scrub. Per-chip speed factor. Idle float ±6px after settle. Cream surface #FAF7F0.
Horizontal pillarsGSAP timeline + ScrollTrigger pin. Pattern from v1 sample: 420vh outer, panels 78vw, linear scrub with dwell gaps between slides (empty timeline beats). Panel content staggers in on enter. Progress dots + label.
After pinRelease with no hitch — marquee and stats continue vertically. No second pin on same page.
MobileNo pin. Pillars stack full-width. Portfolio → 2-col collage. Lenis optional. Nav condense still applies.
SEO / LLMAll pillar copy in DOM as real text. Horizontal track is CSS transform only — not canvas.
Build filehomepage-new.html (prototype). Promote to index.html when approved. Do not break current homepage until sign-off.
06 · Brand guardrails

Keep what works.

v1 sample introduced Fraunces + heavy cream/ink rebrand. v2 stays on the live system. Wolverine gives us stagecraft, not a new palette.

TokenDirection
TypeInter Tight only. 800 for display headlines, 500–600 for nav and body. Tight tracking on large type (-0.03em to -0.04em). Stacked line breaks for hero and pillar titles.
ColorInk default #0A0A0F. White copy sections. One cream band (portfolio) per page view for editorial contrast — not a full cream retheme.
SpectrumExisting rainbow gradient. Max once per viewport: hero "Company." and/or nav load sweep. Signature, not wallpaper.
NavExisting universal-nav flat links (no Partners / Case Studies). Condense behavior as demoed.
PhotographyTeam travel stories + network destinations. Real names in story cards on About — chips are entry points, not decoration.
VoiceConfident, infrastructure-minded, human-first. Category → proof → invite. Avoid generic AI hype.
