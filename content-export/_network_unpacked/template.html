The Network

The network connects travelers, AI agents, and supply providers around a single goal: making the purchase and consumption of travel better for everyone involved. This is the Third Voice in production: agents aligned to the interests travelers and suppliers already share, not a fourth party extracting from all three.

It follows Metcalfe's Law: value compounds as participants join. Every new brand, traveler, or supply partner makes the network more valuable for all the others. Today that starts from 530+ brands and 50M+ travelers a year, so the compounding isn't hypothetical.

V ∝ N²

The Technology

Two models. One portable context.

Our patent pending architecture pairs the Traveler Action Model with the Shared Vector Travel Protocol. Together they carry two of the four pillars: TAM is the memory, SVTP is the trust.

Pillar · Memory
TAM

Traveler Action Model

The memory

A compact, pseudonymous memory of a traveler's preferences. Built from passive, active, analyzed, and enriched signals. Never PII.

  • Privacy first, with no personally identifiable information
  • Learns continuously from traveler signals and outcomes
  • Compact vector representation enables real time matching
  • Self improving as the network observes more behavior
SVTP moves what TAM remembers
Pillar · Trust
SVTP

Shared Vector Travel Protocol

The trust

The standard way to exchange traveler memory between partners. Consent based sharing lets the receiving site personalize without ever seeing PII.

  • Consent based model keeps travelers in control
  • Warm handoffs preserve full context
  • Works across brands, partners, and AI agents
  • An industry standard protocol for the agentic age

The Mechanism

Everything becomes a vector.

Portable traveler context rests on vectorization: a numerical representation where closeness means similarity. Every item becomes a point in multi dimensional space.
