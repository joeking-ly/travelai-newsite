Agentic AI without process optimization, orchestration will flop

Discover how agentic AI and process automation impact the travel industry by enhancing personalization and boosting operational efficiency.

JV
Joshua Viner
Published Nov 28, 2024 • 3 minutes

Agentic AI is rapidly becoming a transformative force in enterprise technology, as eloquently discussed in Larry Dignan’s article. While much of the conversation has centered around its impact on general business processes, the travel industry stands to gain significantly from these developments. By integrating agentic AI with process automation and orchestration, travel companies can benefit greatly.

Unlocking Seamless Travel Experiences with Agentic AI

In the travel industry, customer expectations revolve around personalization, speed, and convenience. Eventually, agentic AI will autonomously handle tasks such as booking flights, hotels. While creating customized itineraries is already here, adapting to real-time changes like delays or cancellations is being built out. As Dignan points out, without robust process automation and orchestration, these AI agents may remain confined within data silos, limiting their effectiveness.

Breaking Down Data Silos for Enhanced Personalization

Travel companies often grapple with fragmented data spread across booking systems, customer service platforms, and third-party vendors. By employing a neutral platform with connectors to multiple systems travel businesses can unify their data. This integration enables AI agents to access comprehensive customer profiles, leading to highly personalized and efficient service delivery.

Process Mining and Optimization: The Backbone of Agentic AI

For agentic AI to truly excel, it must be underpinned by optimized processes. Tools that offer process mining and optimization can help travel companies identify bottlenecks in their operations. Platforms like SAP’s DataSphere/Signavio allow for the refinement of workflows, ensuring that AI agents operate in an efficient framework.

Orchestrating AI Agents for Superior Service

As the number of AI agents increases, orchestration becomes critical. In the travel industry, this means coordinating various AI agents responsible for different aspects of the customer journey—from initial search queries to post-trip feedback. Effective orchestration ensures these agents work in harmony, providing a seamless experience that feels both personalized and intuitive.

Embracing the Future of Travel with Agentic AI

The integration of agentic AI into the travel industry is not without challenges. Companies must be wary of potential pitfalls like agent sprawl and ensure they are not locked into single-vendor solutions that limit flexibility. By focusing on platforms that offer horizontal integration and robust orchestration capabilities, travel businesses can harness the full potential of agentic AI.

In Appreciation of Forward-Thinking Insights

Larry Dignan’s exploration provides invaluable perspectives that resonate deeply with the travel industry’s aspirations. His emphasis on process optimization and orchestration serves as a guide for travel companies eager to stay ahead in a competitive landscape.
