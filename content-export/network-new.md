The Mechanism

## Everything becomes a vector.

Portable traveler context rests on vectorization — a numerical representation where closeness means similarity. Every item becomes a point in multi-dimensional space.

**Step 01 — Raw Signal**
Traveler behavior, preferences, and intent, captured across touchpoints.

**Step 02 — Embedding Model**
A neural network turns raw data into dense numerical representations.

**Step 03 — TAM Vector**
A compact, pseudonymous representation — no PII, fully portable.

**Step 04 — Match & Rank**
Cosine similarity finds the closest items in real time, across millions of properties.

### Vector space: closeness = similarity

One format lets every entity be compared, combined, and retrieved in real time — without exposing raw data.

- **Travelers** — clustered by preference profile and travel style
- **Properties** — beach resorts, city hotels, boutique stays
- **Activities** — experiences matched by vibe and context
- **Locations** — destinations with personality embeddings

Raw Data "Beach trip" → Embed() → TAM Vector [0.12, -0.88…] → Match: Top-K similar
