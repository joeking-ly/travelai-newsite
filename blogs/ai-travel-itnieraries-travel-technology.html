As travel companies continue to seek innovative solutions to meet the high expectations of today’s travelers, the findings discussed by the author are crucial for shaping future strategies that effectively integrate AI without compromising the quality and reliability of travel experiences.

One area we see missing from many of the LLMs is the fine-tuning required to limit hallucinations and improve accuracy. We don’t know what data sets are being used in the training of these LLMs, but we know that training models with relevant data sources in the travel industry are likely to produce better results.
