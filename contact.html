Common questions

Who is TravelAI? +
TravelAI, known as The Travel Memory Company, is an AI infrastructure company for the travel industry. Incorporated in Canada and a subsidiary of UpNext Ventures Inc., TravelAI is founder-owned and has been profitable since inception. Its mission is to create better travel for one billion travelers by 2030. TravelAI operates an owned-distribution network of more than 530 travel brands, serving over 50 million travelers a year in six languages across 200 countries. Its platform includes Traveler.md, a traveler-owned portable memory layer that lets any service read a traveler's preferences and trips with permission, and Tripp, a multimodal AI agent built to help create better travel memories for everyone.
How do I become a TravelAI partner? +
Reach out through our partnerships@travelai.com or the form on this page. We work with travel brands, OTAs, accommodation providers, and tech partners—our team will get back to you to explore fit and integration options.
What types of partnerships does TravelAI offer? +
We offer distribution partnerships (traffic and bookings), technology integrations (API, SVTP, TAM), and co-marketing opportunities. Email partnerships@travelai.com or use the form on this page to learn how we work with brands and platforms.
Is TravelAI hiring? +
Yes. We're growing across engineering, product, growth, and operations. We hire remote-first and our team spans the globe, though we're proud to be building from Canada, where strong federal support for homegrown technology helps us invest in talent and grow at home. We're looking for high-agency, AI-first people who take ownership and build. Follow us on LinkedIn for current openings and new roles as they open.
Where is TravelAI based? +
We're headquartered in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada, and incorporated in Canada. We're remote-first, so our team works from around the world to build and operate our global travel AI network.
How can I learn more about TravelAI's technology? +
Explore our Platform and Insights pages for technical overviews, documentation, and insights. For deeper technical discussions, get in touch and we can arrange a call.
