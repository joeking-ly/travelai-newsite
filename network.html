The Network

The network connects travelers, AI agents, and supply providers around a single goal: making the purchase and consumption of travel better for everyone involved. This is the Third Voice in production: agents aligned to the interests travelers and suppliers already share, not a fourth party extracting from all three. Portable context travels with the traveler and moves between network participants only with consent, so a receiving brand personalizes on arrival without ever seeing PII.

It follows Metcalfe's Law. Value compounds as participants join: every new brand, traveler, supply partner, or AI Agent makes the network more valuable for all the others. When you align an infinite number of AI Agents to a shared common outcome, the speed at which a network can grow is exponential.

V ∝ N²

The Mechanism

Everything becomes a vector.

Portable traveler context rests on vectorization: a numerical representation where closeness means similarity. Every item becomes a point in multi-dimensional space.

Step 01

Raw
Signal

Traveler behavior, preferences, and intent, captured across touchpoints.

Step 02

Embedding
Model

A neural network turns raw data into dense numerical representations.

Step 03

TAM
Vector

A compact, pseudonymous representation. No PII, fully portable.

Step 04

Match &
Rank

Cosine similarity finds the closest items in real time, across millions of properties.
