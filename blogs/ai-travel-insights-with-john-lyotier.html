The Slick Talk Podcast is for anyone and everyone in the hospitality industry who loves what they do and wants to grow, learn, and experience all the cool things in the world of hotels, restaurants, vacation rentals, and travel offers! On the show, Wil Slickers interviews some of the biggest founders and professionals within all hospitality segments.

This episode features an intriguing discussion with John Lyotier, the founder of TravelAI.com. The conversation dives into the origins and entrepreneurial journey of John, from his early days as a child entrepreneur in the dot com era to his venture into startups, focusing on the intersection of marketing and technology.

The narrative unfolds to reveal TravelAI‘s recent acquisition of the domain Casai, expanding its footprint in the Latin American property management market. Highlighting the transition during the COVID-19 pandemic, John shares insights on starting anew with TravelAI.com by leveraging AI and domain expertise in the travel industry. The episode touches on AI’s potential to enhance personalization and efficiency in travel and discusses the evolving landscape of online travel marketing. Listeners are offered a glimpse into future endeavors with AI, focusing on the development of their TripBot initiative, which aims to transform travel informational accessibility and customer engagement.
