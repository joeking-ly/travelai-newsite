Listen to Everything AI in Travel’s Tony Carne as he has a conversation with Dan Flores, VP of Sales for Satisfi Labs, a conversational AI engagement platform. With a long career in travel, learn more about what Dan has seen over the past year in conversational AI and what excites him about what is coming next. In addition to time at Satisfi Labs, Dan’s extensive experience includes being part of the National Advisory Council at the National Blacks in Travel & Tourism Collaborative and the Technology Task Force at the International Inbound Travel Association. Flores’ career path also includes a significant tenure (and in his words, “lots of ups and downs”) as VP of Business Development at Maverick Helicopters, showcasing his broad expertise in hospitality and tourism.
